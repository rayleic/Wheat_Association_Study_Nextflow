Find the HapMap genotype data at https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1pkEbUroEfgUwlSCCrij-HuStXgFetOyY?usp=sharing   

Data: Panel of 828 wheat accessions originally gathered in 1930 by A.E. Watkin from many regions in Europe, Asia and Africa. 
Genotyped on ThermoFischer’s Axiom 35k SNP chip. 35100 SNP markers tested.
Data from Wingen et al. 2014 modified and given to Andy Chen (John Innes Centre) for his lesson
